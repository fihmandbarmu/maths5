🍌 Bento's Decimal Division Academy (Age 8+)

An interactive, cheerful, and highly visual math learning web application designed to teach kids (Ages 8 and up) how to divide numbers by 2, 5, 10, and 100 resulting in decimal outputs (e.g. 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5, 45 ÷ 10 = 4.5, 342 ÷ 100 = 3.42).

✨ Core Features

  • Bento the Banana Mascot: Animated SVG mascot with dynamic speech bubbles, audio voice synthesis, cheerful reactions, and custom unlockable hats!
  • Interactive Place Value Visualizer: Demonstrates decimal point shifts visually in real-time.
  • Practice Arena: On-screen touch keypad, instant step-by-step hint breakdown, and customized practice pools (÷2, ÷5, ÷10, ÷100, or Mixed).
  • Banana Rush Arcade Mode: 45-second fast-paced quiz challenge rewarding speed and precision with star points.
  • Wardrobe & Locker System: Gamified star rewards system for unlocking custom accessories for Bento.

📐 Question Generation & Division Rules

1. Dividing by 2 (Finding Half)

Rule: Halve the number. If odd, result ends in .5.

Example: 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5

2. Dividing by 5 (Double & Shift Left 1)

Rule: Double the number first, then shift the decimal point 1 place to the left!

Example: 12 ÷ 5 ➔ Double 12 = 24 ➔ Shift left = 2.4

3. Dividing by 10 (Shift Left 1)

Rule: Move the decimal point 1 spot to the left.

Example: 45 ÷ 10 = 4.5

4. Dividing by 100 (Shift Left 2)

Rule: Move the decimal point 2 spots to the left.

Example: 342 ÷ 100 = 3.42
